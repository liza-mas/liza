  inside the same ownership task lock or project lifecycle lock. Private steps

  no lock. Pre-lock checks and their order (replace: request identity,

  project recorded completion evidence (`replacementCompletionConsumers`, the
  receipt projection), never today's graph (`scope` CONTRACT).
  `time.Now().UTC()` stays at its call points. No new mutation site

  `failed to cancel task: %w`, `failed to delete task: %w`,
