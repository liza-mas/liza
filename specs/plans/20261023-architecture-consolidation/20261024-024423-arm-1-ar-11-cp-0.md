   sanitized excerpts. A failed G1–G4 goes through `mark-blocked`, naming the
   check, A1, revision, exact command/source and failure; never repair it here.

   checked out at the observed integration SHA, outside task worktrees. Use
   `env -i HOME="$HOME" PATH="$PATH" TMPDIR=/tmp GOFLAGS=-count=1`, one

   exit codes, host/Go version and load. For each of `./internal/db`,
   `./internal/commands`, `./internal/agent`, use the baseline's
   `timeout 300 go test -run '^$' -bench . -benchmem <package>`.
   Compare every benchmark/sub-benchmark to baseline or record "not captured"
   with reason; retain ns/op, B/op and allocs/op as applicable.
   Report wall-time delta and the +25% comparison, explicitly qualifying the
   red 523 s baseline and the distinct green 444 s warm-up: do not silently
   substitute one for the other. Give every slowdown, out-of-bound result,
   missing result or measurement failure a disposition; G5 never blocks.
   Review the registry summary and candidate sections against merged A1 changes.
   Record the expected no-closure judgment for issue-vocab and issue-status.
   Close a different wholly resolved item only with actual A1 commit evidence,
   updating the active section, Completed Fixes, Fixed (Traceability), Fix Details
   as needed, and Open Issues Summary per issues-policy.
   Create G1–G8 sections in boundary-a1.md. Each holds its command/source,
   integration SHA and excerpt; G8 is explicitly pending until supplied.
4. **Obtain G8 and finish.** Preserve the G1–G7 work in a task commit before
   `mark-blocked --human-action` asks the maintainer to open the
   `global-refactoring`→`main` PR and record Linux/Windows CI results for
   the tested integration revision. Include the SHA and exact requested
   evidence in the human action. Stop after the lifecycle command directs
   stop; do not submit a pending-G8 artifact for completion.
   On authorized resume with green evidence, append the PR URL/number, tested
   full SHA, Linux/Windows check names, URLs/results and UTC observation.
   Record macOS as the subsequent `main`-push check prescribed by goal-proof;
   never claim it ran before that push. On red CI the maintainer commissions
   the fix and the next area remains held. If CI or a rebase tests different
   code, retain the distinction and refresh affected G1–G7 evidence at the
   new integration revision before claiming closure; green CI from another
   revision is insufficient. Complete the acceptance manifest, pre-commit all
   touched files, verify only owned files changed, commit, submit the captured
   immutable submission SHA and follow the documented verdict lifecycle.

No remote push, provider CLI invocation, installation/update, live runtime-state
