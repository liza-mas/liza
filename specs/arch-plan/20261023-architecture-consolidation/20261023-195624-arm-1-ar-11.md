## Goal

Close area A1 through its boundary checkpoint, then bring A2 under S1 by separating the concerns
of its eight offenders inside their own packages, and make A2's finite control-flow vocabulary
typed and its magic values named, with every A2 artifact and command surface unchanged.

  arises. In `internal/embedded`, new files are top-level `internal/embedded/*.go`: `.gitignore`

- **BC4 Offender path kept.** A file offender keeps its path and a cohesive remaining concern and
  ends at or under the threshold in the task that removes its ratchet entry; no offender is renamed
  or deleted while over threshold (`arch-size`).

## Design Summary

Five scopes: the A1 boundary unit (Scope 1), three concern-map scopes for A2's offenders grouped by
domain (Scopes 2–4), and A2's typed vocabulary with named values (Scope 5). Splitting first and
typing after keeps each review a pure move or a pure retyping, as in A1. Each Scope carries its
own design so its child acts from the Scope alone; BC1–BC4 bind Scopes 2–5.

## Interfaces

**A2 → later areas.** A2's exported API is unchanged by Scopes 2–4. Scope 5 changes it only by
the W-table carriers and the removal of the two exported truthy parsers; callers in A3–A9 follow by
conversion or constant naming (X2). Direction: A3–A9, `cmd/liza` import A2; A2 imports only
infrastructure, domain and leaf packages (RP3).

**A2 → oracle adapters.** A2 oracle adapters (`a2-oracle-artifact`, `a2-oracle-cli`) and any other
oracle adapter compiling against a changed A2 API adapt by call-site-only edits under the adapter
rule (`arch-harness`), with no golden, fixture or `PROVENANCE` change.

**Provider asks.** No epic stage and no merged consumer cites A2's vocabulary; the master names
later areas as adopters (X7). A9 is the known adopter of W2/W3 (its untyped `agent` constants,
RP10); it raises a missing value as a correction to this plan's owner.
